# Vimla Designer Jewellery

A polished, responsive storefront for **Vimla Designer Jewellery**, showcasing Indian jewellery curated in Kota, Rajasthan.

## Brand

- Official logo: `logo.svg`
- Brand name: Vimla Designer Jewellery
- Style: Rajasthani-inspired, elegant and contemporary

## Website menu

- Home
- Collections (dropdown)
- Shop Jewellery
- Our Craft
- About Us
- Contact
- Search
- Shopping bag and WhatsApp order request

## Product categories

- Bridal Collection: Rajputi Rani Haar, Kundan Bridal Jhumkas, Borla Maang Tikka, bridal chokers, Hathphool and Mangalsutra sets.
- Brass & Copper: Antique chokers, necklaces, Rani Haar, Kamarband, earrings, Bali, rings, bangles, bracelets and pendants.
- Temple Jewellery: Temple necklaces, Lakshmi necklaces, pendants, bangles, Kamarband and traditional Jadau earrings.
- 1–2 Gram Gold-Plated: Lightweight necklaces, chokers, bangles, rings, earrings, pendants, bracelets, Mangalsutra and ready-stock sets.
- Rajasthani Heritage: Rani Haar, Borla, Maang Tikka, Aad necklaces, Hathphool, Kamarband, Nath, Rakhdi and bridal sets.
- Daily Wear: Mangalsutra, lightweight earrings, Bali sets, simple rings, bangles, pendants and chains.

Suggested bangle sizes: 2x4, 2x6, 2x8 and 2x10.
